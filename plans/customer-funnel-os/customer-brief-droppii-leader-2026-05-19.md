## Product Customer Audience

End users cover the whole family:

- Children from 3 years old and above.
- Adults and older family members.
- Family health and household wellness use cases.

Purchase decision maker:

- Primary buyer: women from 28 to U50.
- Messaging should speak to the woman managing family health, daily routines, and household purchase decisions.
- Avoid over-narrowing content to only young women, weight loss, or beauty. The umbrella positioning is family healthspan and proactive wellness.

## Recruitment Audience

Recruitment target:

- Women U30, U40, U50.
- Later expansion: students.

Recruitment messaging should connect business opportunity with real product belief:

- From family user to sharer.
- From health-conscious woman to community builder.
- From personal/family transformation to trusted referrer.

## Medicine 3.0 Positioning

Medicine 3.0 is a modern medical approach popularized by Dr. Peter Attia, author of *Outlive*.

Core idea:

- Prevent disease before it occurs instead of only treating symptoms after they appear.
- Shift focus from lifespan only to healthspan: the length of time a person lives in good health.

Positioning ladder:

- Medicine 1.0: ancient medicine based on observation and guesswork.
- Medicine 2.0: modern reactive medicine, treating disease after it appears.
- Medicine 3.0: proactive, preventive, personalized health practice focused on extending healthspan.

Application to Droppii messaging:

- Do not claim disease treatment or cure.
- Use language such as "chăm sóc sức khỏe chủ động", "phòng ngừa từ thói quen", "sống khỏe lâu hơn", "kéo dài thời gian sống khỏe mạnh", and "healthspan".
- Product content should be framed as daily routine support, nutrition, environment, sleep, energy, digestion, and family wellness.

## Funnel Implications

- L0 lead magnets should include family healthspan quiz, Medicine 3.0 explainer, and proactive wellness checklist.
- L1 trial products should be positioned as easy first steps for a family routine.
- L2/L3 journeys should emphasize 30-90 day health habit tracking, not aggressive medical outcomes.
- L4 partner invite should target women who already believe in proactive family health and want to share it.

## Compliance Guardrails

- No disease cure claims.
- No guaranteed treatment outcomes.
- No income guarantees for recruitment.
- Use education-first framing: proactive care, healthy routine, family wellness, and healthspan.
