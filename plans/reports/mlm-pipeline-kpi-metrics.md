## 2. Sales Funnel Stages (Typical MLM)

Standard 5-stage funnel used by network marketing sales orgs:

```
PROSPECT → CONTACTED → PRESENTED → CUSTOMER → DISTRIBUTOR
(pres.)    (qual.)     (demo)    (1st sale)  (recruited)
```

---

## 3. Conversion Rate Benchmarks by Stage

| Funnel Stage | Typical MLM Conversion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Contacted → Presented | 20–40% | Warm leads / events vs cold |
| Presented → Customer | 1st purchase | 10–25% of presentations become first sale |
| Customer → Repeat Customer | 20–35% within 90 days | Loyalty product or auto-ship |
| Customer → Distributor Sponsor | 1–5% | Only motivated top customers |
| Prospect → Qualified (no action) | 60–80% drop-off | Normal for network marketing |

### Overall Contact-to-Customer Rate
- **10–15%** of initial contacts become paying customers (industry average)
- **2–5%** of contacts eventually recruit into the team

## 4. Average Time in Each Funnel Stage

| Stage | Avg. Time (Days) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Contacted → Presented | < 3 days | Fast initial contact critical |
| Presented → Decision | 3–14 days | Product demo / meeting follow-up |
| Decision → 1st Purchase | 0–7 days | Usually same session for impulse buys |
| Customer → Repeat Order | 14–30 days | Average refill cycle |
| Customer → Distributor | 45–180 days | Longer relationship building |
| Full cycle (prospect → distributor) | 90–365 days | Dependent on team strength |

## 6. Key Pipeline KPIs to Track

### A. Volume / Lead Generation
| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| New contacts per period | Count of new leads | W/M: +10% MoM |
| Contact conversion rate | Contacts → Presented / Contacts | ≥ 25% |
| Pipeline coverage | Total leads in funnel / Monthly target | ≥ 3x |

### B. Conversion Efficiency
| KPI | Formula | Industry Avg |
|---|---|---|
| Overall funnel conversion | Customers / Total contacts | 10–15% |
| Presentation-to-close | Sales / Presentations | 10–25% |
| Lead-to-sale cycle | Avg days from contact to sale | 7–21 days |
| Customer retention rate | Repeat purchasers / All customers | 20–40% at 90 days |
| Reactivation rate | Re-engaged lapsed customers | 5–10% quarterly |

### C. Team / Distributor Productivity
| KPI | Formula | Good / Avg |
|---|---|---|
| Avg sales per distributor/month | Revenue / Active distributors | $200–$500 |
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | Marketing spend / New customers | < 30% of AOV |
| Distributor-on-distributor activation | Active distributors / Total distributors | > 25% monthly |
| Recruitment ratio | New distributors / Total team | 2–5% monthly active growth |
| Team depth utilization | % of team levels with ≥ 1 active | 60–80% |

### D. Revenue & Growth
| KPI | Formula | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) | Sum active auto-ship | Stable ±15% MoM |
| Lifetime value (LTV) | Avg revenue over customer lifespan | 3x–8x AOV |
| Churn rate | Lost distributors / Total | 30–50% monthly (normal!) |
| Order value growth | Current AOV / Previous AOV | +5–10% YoY |
| Volume points / PV | Team product movement | 100–500 PV/month per active |

## 7. Funnel Health Indicators (Dashboard Headlines)

### Green (Healthy)

## 8. Leading vs Lagging Indicators

**Leading** (predict future):
- New contacts/week
- Presentations scheduled
- Follow-up completion rate
- Activity score per distributor

**Lagging** (confirm outcomes):
- Revenue per distributor
- Customer retention rate
- Team growth rate
- Monthly bonuses paid

---

## 9. Team-Level Benchmarking

For a healthy MLM company, typical team performance distribution:

| Tier | % of Team | Activity Level | Revenue Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top 5% | Builders / Leaders | 80% activity | 50% of sales |
| Next 15% | Active earners | 40–80% activity | 30% of sales |
| Middle 30% | Semi-active | 10–40% activity | 15% of sales |
| Bottom 50% | Passive / inactive | < 10% activity | 5% of sales |

**Rule of thumb:** Only ~20–30% of distributors are truly active in any given month.

## 11. Key Formulas for Dashboard Calculations

```
Pipeline Coverage = Σ(Funnel Stage Value) / Monthly Target
Funnel Value = ∑(#leads × stage_value_weight)

Conversion Rate Stage = (Completed stage / Entered stage) × 100

Pipeline Velocity = (Total leads moved forward) / (Pipeline entries)
                  = stages/day metric for bull whip analysis

Distributor Efficiency = Monthly Sales / Active Distributors

Activation Rate = Active Distributors / Total Registered Distributors

Retention Rate = (EOC Customers - New Customers) / BOC Customers × 100
```

## Unresolved Questions

1. **Company-specific TAM not confirmed**: Recommend company's distributor count (active/total) and current monthly sales figures.
2. **Social media vs offline lead channels**: MLM varies significantly by channel; social-media-first companies see different conversion curves (higher contact rate, lower close rate on cold).
3. **Product category impact**: Health/wellness MLM ≠ beauty MLM ≠ financial services MLM in stage-duration profiles.
4. **Compensation plan effect**: Binary/unilevel/matrix plans produce different team velocity and retention patterns not captured in generic benchmarks.
5. **Geo-specific benchmarks**: Vietnamese/SEA MLM market differs from US/EU benchmarks; local commission structure revelation was QUIZZED by EU regulator (2012 ruling on corporate authorship).
