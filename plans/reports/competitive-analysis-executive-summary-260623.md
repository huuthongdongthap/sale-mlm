## Product-Market Fit Validation

### Core Assumptions to Test (G0 Pilot)

| Assumption | Test | Pass Threshold |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Price Sensitivity | Manual funnel at 590K VND | ≥4 orders from 50 contacts (8%) |
| Tech Adoption (elderly) | Telegram mini-app usage | ≥70% of pilot complete without help |
| AI Coaching Trust | Session completion rate | ≥60% of leads complete AI coach |
| Network Effects | Referral rate | ≥10% of pilot participants refer others |
| Compliance Viability | Legal review | No blocking regulatory barriers |

**Pilot Design:** 50 warm contacts (10 friends + 20 old customers + 20 CTV), 4 weeks, manual Zalo DM (no automation), budget 1.5M VND.

**Decision Gates:**
- **G0 PASS:** ≥2 orders from 10 friends → proceed to automated funnel build
- **G6 PASS:** 3/5 KPIs met → Phase 2 (scale + paid traffic)
- **FAIL:** Pivot or stop
